# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Privacy-sensitive PDF operators are the primary users. Prepress,
accessibility, OCR, forms, signing, AEC, review, and support specialists use the
workbench to inspect evidence and run bounded local operations. Occasional
users need the same trust model explained without losing access to expert
detail.

## Product Purpose

Platen is a local proof desk for opening an immutable PDF source,
inspecting source-bound evidence, navigating and searching pages, and producing
separate validated artifacts through explicit bounded operations. Success means
the operator can always distinguish the unchanged source, current evidence,
available local action, derived result, and an unavailable or indeterminate
claim.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, accountable. The interface should feel like a professional
prepress inspection instrument: dense where comparison benefits from density,
quiet where the operator needs focus, and direct about every trust boundary.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards, promotional feature grids, and decorative metrics.
- Acrobat imitation that implies unsupported parity.
- Consumer document editors that blur preview, source, and saved mutation.
- Glassmorphism, gradients, oversized rounded cards, ornamental motion, and
  vague smart or AI language.
- Marketplace UI that makes non-executable plugin skeletons look installable.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence and task clarity before decoration.
2. Source immutability and output consequences stay visible.
3. Expert density uses stable hierarchy, not visual noise.
4. Planned, blocked, and indeterminate states remain explicit.
5. One shared interface vocabulary spans every route and responsive state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, semantic landmarks,
screen-reader labels, non-color state cues, 200% zoom and reflow, reduced-motion
preferences, and a usable 390px review viewport. Dense operational text must
remain legible and errors must identify a recovery path.
